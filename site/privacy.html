Privacy policy

Updated 26 November 2026.

"Line & Light Publisher" is a private tool used only by the owner of the Line & Light and «خط وضوء» channels. It uploads the owner's own short videos to the owner's own YouTube channels and, through Buffer, to the owner's own Instagram accounts.

What it accesses

Only permission to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channels (scope youtube.upload). It does not read, collect, store or share any data about viewers or other users.

Where data is kept

The access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's GitHub repository and are used only to publish the owner's videos. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Through the channels' profiles on Instagram.
